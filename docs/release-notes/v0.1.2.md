## Fn Cheatsheet v0.1.2 — new Settings

**Settings, redesigned** in the style of Windows 11 Settings — still drawn by the app itself (Direct2D), no UI framework:
- Sidebar navigation (Device · Trigger & display · Updates & about), card sections
- Toggle switches, segmented buttons and sliders instead of checkboxes and drop-downs
- Model list with two-line rows and badges (This PC, Partly verified, My file); raw device strings behind "Show details"
- Dark and light themes with a matching title bar; long titles end with "…"

**Data**
- ASUS ExpertBook P1 / B1 15": **Fn + F → fan mode** (Standard / Whisper / Performance). The E-Manual's hotkey table leaves it out; it is confirmed on a P1503CVA ([#3](https://github.com/jeiel85/cheatsheet-win/issues/3)). Other ExpertBooks get it once someone confirms on their model.
