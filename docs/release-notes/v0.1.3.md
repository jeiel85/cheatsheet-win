## Fn Cheatsheet v0.1.3 — double tap & FnProbe

- **Double-tap trigger**: tap Left Ctrl twice quickly to show the sheet; it stays until the next key press or click. Settings → Trigger & display → *How*: Hold / Double tap / Both. Ctrl+click twice (multi-select) and slow taps don't trigger it.
- **More reliable key handling**: the hook thread now uses a plain GetMessage loop with a timer armed from the hook itself. The previous wait loop could miss hook callbacks, so key-ups were sometimes seen late or not at all.
- **FnProbe.exe** (new download): a read-only diagnostic that checks every channel Windows offers when Fn is pressed alone — keyboard hook, raw HID (vendor pages included), vendor WMI events, and on ASUS a noise-filtered ACPI status scan. Settings links to it. If it finds a signal on your laptop, open an issue and that model can use Fn itself.
